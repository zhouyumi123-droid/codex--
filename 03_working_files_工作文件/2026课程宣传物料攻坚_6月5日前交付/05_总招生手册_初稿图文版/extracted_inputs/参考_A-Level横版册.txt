The school is located at No. 2040 Buxin Road, Luohu District, Shenzhen.Guided by the educational philosophy of "Student-Centered, Character First, Tailored Instruction, Diverse Development," the school aims to "Keep Pace with the World and Cultivate Cross-Generation Elite Tal- ents." Leveraging its strength in foreign language education, it offers a diverse range of programs to meet the needs of Shenzhen’s international community, partic- ularly the children of overseas investors and entrepreneurs. Developed under the official authorization of both Edexcel and AQA examination boards, this program combines academic excellence with personal development to prepare students for successful entry into leading universities in the UK, Hong Kong, Australia, and other international destinations. While A-Level qualifications are primarily aligned with UK and Commonwealth university systems, they are also widely accepted by top U.S. institutions, including Ivy League schools, when paired with competitive academic performance, strong English proficiency, and comprehensive applications. At OXSTAND, students benefit from interdisciplinary coursework, small class sizes, certified teaching staff, and individualized university counseling — all tailored to nurture future-ready, globally minded leaders.
